Drizzy is running at roughly half a million dollars a year in gross revenue, five months after its first bottle shipped. That is brand revenue: what Drizzy bills its distributor plus what shoppers pay it online. Measured as money spent at the shelf, the same business is closer to $0.75M.

Two channels carry almost all of it. Sprouts Farmers Market lists it in 483 US stores, a chain-wide launch on August 24. TikTok Shop US opened on September 3 and has run at about $580 a day since. Australia is small by comparison: 32 stores and an online store that sold out its first production run in April.

They got here with no paid digital advertising, two viral TikTok recipe videos, a pre-seed round, and a team of two. The founders have never published revenue, units or the round size. Every number here is either pulled from a public record or built from one with the arithmetic shown.

The number that decides the next year is how many bottles each Sprouts store sells per week. Sprouts guarantees a new item six months on shelf, so its first real keep-or-cut decision lands around February 2027.

If Sprouts sells at a launch pace of 3.5 bottles per store per week, which the current sweepstakes and $1.50 price cut could produce for a few weeks, the Sprouts row becomes $470K and the total approaches $1M. Treat that as the ceiling for the promotion window, not a steady state.

Why other estimates may look bigger

  • Shelf dollars versus brand dollars. A bottle sells at Sprouts for $11.49 regular ($9.99 on promotion now), but Drizzy bills its distributor $5.35. Shelf figures run about 2.1 times brand revenue: base-case Sprouts sales are $433K at the shelf but $202K to Drizzy.
  • Gross versus net. Everything here is gross. Sprouts takes the first case per store free (about $15.5K), and trade promotions, distributor fees and TikTok commissions all come off before Drizzy sees cash.
  • The sales-per-store assumption. Each extra bottle per store per week at Sprouts adds about $134K a year to Drizzy, or $289K at the shelf. It is the only input that moves the total by a lot.

United States: nearly all of Sprouts, nothing else

Drizzy’s own store locator lists 500 Sprouts addresses and no other US retailer. Sprouts reported 490 stores after Q2 with more opening since, so the listing covers essentially the whole chain. The founders round it to “500+ Sprouts Farmers Market stores”.

Every Sprouts store was then checked individually through Sprouts’ own online shop. Of 499 locations, 483 in all 25 states list Drizzy, every one at $9.99 on promotion from $11.49. None shows as low or out of stock three weeks after launch. 16 stores don’t carry it, including Plano, Murphy and Bastrop in Texas and Costa Mesa in California; a control product worked at all 16. The brand’s locator is messier than it looks: 4 of its addresses are Sprouts distribution centres and 4 match no open store.

Open question

None of the top 20 creator videos in Euka’s data is credited with a sale, yet sales held at ~$580 a day from day two. They are coming from somewhere else: TikTok’s own shop ads (which appear in no public ad library), shop search, Drizzy’s own posts, or its four livestreams. Paid promotion is unconfirmed.

Named investors

The pre-seed was announced July 28 by Drizzy’s law firm, Foley & Lardner, backed by RiverPark Ventures, Nucleus Ventures and Alexis Ohanian. No amount was given anywhere. Three public records narrow it down:

  • An SEC filing for a single-deal investor pool. “Drizzy SPV Apr 2026” raised $66,875 from 13 accredited investors (first sale April 28). Nucleus Ventures runs deals this way and lists Drizzy in its portfolio, so it is most likely theirs.
  • The lead’s stated cheque size. RiverPark Ventures says its first cheque is $500K to $1.5M, and lists Drizzy in its portfolio.
  • RiverPark’s office on the import paperwork. Drizzy’s US company received its first shipment addressed to 156 W 56th St, 17th floor, New York, which is RiverPark’s office. That points to RiverPark as lead.

RiverPark’s minimum plus the pool plus Ohanian gives more than about $0.57M, which makes $0.6M to $2M the plausible band. It is an estimate, not a disclosed figure.

Supply chain

  • US bottles: one container of empty bottles and caps from Xiamen, China, landed June 10 (3,567 kg, 332 cartons), roughly 75,000 to 130,000 bottles at an assumed weight each. That is many times the Sprouts stocking order.
  • US product: an unnamed co-packer, “100% American ingredients”, Texas-grown Runner peanuts per Founder Gazette.
  • Australian peanuts: from Kingaroy, Queensland. Manufacturer not named.

Trademarks

  • The US name is on hold. DRIZZY was suspended July 1. Garnea LLC filed DRIZZ for nut butters three weeks before Drizzy and is the likely cause (the examiner’s letter was unreadable).
  • No new product lines. Every filing in both countries covers nut butters only.

The viral moment

The press line of “more than 7.5 million TikTok views” is essentially one video: the strawberry stars recipe, posted March 15, with 7.6M views, 769K likes and 113K shares. It went viral during Australian pre-orders, a month before any bottle shipped. The follow-up rice cake stacks reached 2.6M. No brand video since has passed 180K.

It spread by accident. The strawberries looked like the Stranger Things Demogorgon: the top comment has 26.8K likes, and 265 of 419 sampled comments tag a friend. Only 8 asked what the product was. The reach landed the Sprouts buyer but not a lasting audience: 3,053 followers against 951K likes.

What to watch

The launch is funded and on shelf. The next six months decide whether it stays there.

Risks

  • Sprouts sales after the promotion. The sweepstakes ends September 20 and the price cut is temporary. By about February 2027 the item should be heading toward the 2.4 condiment average; from a 1.5 base that is a 60% lift, with no paid ads and no US pixel.
  • The viral engine has stalled. March and April produced 83% of all TikTok views, and TikTok Shop sales aren’t coming from creator videos yet.
  • The US name. The DRIZZY trademark is suspended, with an earlier “DRIZZ” nut butter filing in the way.
  • Two people. A national US launch, an Australian business, TikTok Shop and all content, run by the founders alone.
  • Cash. A container of bottles, a US co-packer, free product for 483 stores, a billboard and a move to the US, funded by a pre-seed of perhaps $0.6M to $2M.

Cheap checks to repeat

  • Sprouts stock, store by store. A saved script re-checks all 499 stores in about 11 minutes. Run it after September 20 and again two weeks later; stores selling out and restocking are the best public read on real sales.
  • TikTok Shop daily sales on Euka.
  • A first Meta ad on Drizzy’s page, or a Meta pixel appearing on the US store. Either means they’ve started paying for customers.
  • New stores and an Amazon listing. A hidden “Amazon 3PKs” product already exists in the US store.
